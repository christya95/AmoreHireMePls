What the app will never do on its own

  • Nothing leaves this computer without your approval. No application is sent and no message goes out unless you approved that exact package, or approved several jobs at once with limits that still stop at the first unclear result.
  • Nothing is made up. Resume lines and form answers come from your approved facts and base resume. Missing facts become questions for you, never guesses.
  • Every send is written down before it happens and checked afterwards: the right job, the right file name, the confirmation text. Anything unclear becomes "Result unclear, check it" instead of a claimed success.
  • Proof is kept. When the app applies for you it stores two screenshots next to the tailored resume: the filled form right before the final click and the confirmation page afterwards (an unclear result keeps what the site showed instead). They appear on the application page and under Resumes. The confirmation is also saved with your tracker entry.
  • Emergency stop (top right, two clicks) stops all sending and applying immediately. Anything already in progress ends with a recorded result. Nothing is deleted. Clear it when you are ready.
  • Some sites you finish yourself. The app never signs in for you and never gets around a security check or a captcha. After you approve an application, a signed-in LinkedIn form can be filled for you. If the site asks you to sign in, to verify it is you, or to finish a step the app does not fill, you do that part and record the confirmation here.
  • Job ads, web pages and messages are treated as untrusted. Nothing written in them can give the app permission or change what it does.
