The workflow in eight steps

Every job page shows these steps with the current one highlighted and one button for what comes next.
  1. 1
    ImportPaste a posting URL under Jobs, or let discovery find postings from your saved sources.
  2. 2
    QualifyDeterministic rules from your profile (role family, location, employment type, salary floor, exclusions) decide qualified / needs your review / excluded. A model then ranks fit 1 to 5.
  3. 3
    Tailor"Tailor and prepare" starts a worker run. It builds a tailored DOCX (and PDF) from your approved base resume; every bullet must trace back to the base.
  4. 4
    ReviewThe run pauses with a review package: the document, the form answers with their source facts, and where it would be sent. Questions the profile cannot answer are marked "needed".
  5. 5
    AuthorizeYou approve this exact package. The approval is bound to the material hash; change anything and you are asked again.
  6. 6
    SubmitThe worker submits through a supported adapter and verifies the receipt. Sites without one (LinkedIn Easy Apply, Workday, logins) become a handoff you finish yourself and record.
  7. 7
    RecordConfirmed submissions are written to your private GitHub tracker through the sync queue. A sync problem never changes the application outcome.
  8. 8
    OutreachFor employers with a confirmed submission, add in-house recruiters by LinkedIn URL, draft short connection notes, send them yourself and record it.

Safety model, in plain words

  • Nothing leaves this machine without your approval. No application is submitted and no message is sent unless you approved that exact package, or granted a bounded batch scope that still stops at the first unknown outcome.
  • Nothing is invented. Resume bullets and form answers come from your approved facts and base resume. Missing facts become questions for you, never guesses.
  • Every external action is recorded before it happens and verified afterwards: correct requisition, uploaded filename, receipt text. Anything ambiguous becomes "Outcome unknown, check it" instead of a claimed success.
  • Emergency stop (top right, two clicks) blocks every queued submission and message at once. In-flight actions finish into a recorded outcome. Nothing is deleted; clear it when you are ready.
  • LinkedIn handoffs. The app never logs in for you, never bypasses CAPTCHAs or access controls. For LinkedIn (and any site without an adapter) it prepares everything, you apply or send yourself, then you record the confirmation here.
  • Postings, pages and messages are untrusted data. They cannot grant permissions or change instructions.
